What Is Custom Tarot Deck Illustration?

Custom tarot deck illustration is the creation of original artwork for every card in a tarot or oracle deck — the 22 Major Arcana, 56 Minor Arcana, card backs, and packaging. Unlike stock or AI-generated decks, a custom-illustrated deck carries a unified artistic vision, consistent symbolism, and artwork you fully own. With 8+ years of illustration experience and 5000+ completed projects, Mirza Creative designs decks built for successful print runs and Kickstarter campaigns.

How It Works

  1. Discovery call — we define your theme, symbolism, and art style from your references.
  2. Style guide — palette, linework, and 2–3 sample cards approved by you before production.
  3. Full production — all cards illustrated in batches with structured revision rounds.
  4. Print delivery — CMYK files with bleed in your printer's exact specs, plus RGB web versions. Full commercial rights transfer on final payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to illustrate a custom tarot deck?

A full 78-card custom tarot deck typically ranges from $10,000 to $25,000+ depending on art style, complexity, and licensing. Smaller oracle decks cost less. Contact us for a tailored quote.

How long does it take to illustrate a 78-card tarot deck?

A complete deck usually takes 4–9 months including concept art, revisions, and print preparation.

Do you illustrate oracle decks as well as tarot decks?

Yes — oracle decks of any card count, plus card backs, packaging, guidebooks, and Kickstarter campaign art.

Will I get print-ready files?

Yes. Print-ready CMYK files with bleed in your printer's exact dimensions, plus web-ready RGB versions.

Who owns the rights to the artwork?

You do. Full commercial rights transfer on final payment — print, sell, and merchandise worldwide.

Can you match an existing art style?

Yes. We develop a cohesive style guide from your references, approved by you before full production begins.
